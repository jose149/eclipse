Look inspirations so you —or your group— can let your creativity run wild:

Cosmic silver

A dress in metallic or liquid fabric that catches the last light of the sun before darkness. Flowing, column or with a slit. Cosmic glamour in its purest form.

Silver · Metallic

Midnight blue

Silk or satin in deep blue, draped and elegant. Minimal yet dramatic, like the sky just before the eclipse. Pair it with astral gold jewellery.

Night blue

Mediterranean splendour

Sand, off-white, toasted gold. Satin or chiffon that flows with the sea breeze at sunset. Summer decadence done elegantly — the relaxed luxury of facing the end of the world by the coast.

Sand · Off-white

Astral tunic

A flowing tunic-style dress with a goddess drape, in silver, off-white or black. The “beautiful cult” touch — tunics welcome as long as they’re pretty and mystical in just the right measure. Thin-strap sandals or a foot jewel.

Goddess · Ethereal

Shine & sparkle

Sequins, rhinestones, iridescent fabric. Here shine is welcome without limits: apocalyptic psychedelia, reflections that shift with the eclipse light. The more cosmic, the better.

Sparkle · Psychedelia

Mystic new age

Cosmic spirituality, chic edition: ethereal kaftans, chiffons, lunar tones. Solar-guru energy but tasteful. Astral details, amulets, floating layers.

Mystic · New age
80% of the look

Accessories are everything

Sunglasses at night

Yes, sunglasses at sunset and after dark. Metallic cat-eye, silver or gold frames, mirrored lenses. The most “end of the world, but cool” gesture there is.

Celestial jewellery

Moons, stars, suns. Gold or silver, statement yet elegant. Long drop earrings, astral necklaces, cosmic rings. The detail that ties the whole concept together.

EoW says yes to:

  • Elegant looks with a strange twist
  • Sunglasses at night
  • Shine and sparkle
  • Astral and futuristic details
  • Apocalyptic psychedelia
  • New age spirituality
  • Cult tunics — pretty and mystical in just the right measure

EoW says no to:

  • Full costumes
  • Over-the-top makeup (there’ll be many of you and few mirrors)
  • Masks
  • A plain beachy or summery look: we’re after something theatrical, creative and memorable — but subtle

The end of the world,
but in good taste.
